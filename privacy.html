Privacy policy

Effective date: 17 October 2026 · Publisher: KoBu
App: Bombloom: Block Puzzle (formerly Chain Reaction: Block Puzzle) · Android package: com.chainreactionstudio.blast

Information the app collects

The current version of Bombloom: Block Puzzle does not collect or transmit personal information to KoBu or third parties. It does not include analytics, tracking, real advertising, in-app purchases or online accounts. It does not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone or advertising identifier.

Progress and preferences

Your high score, round scores, experience, level, refresh credits, tutorial progress and sound, theme, language and vibration preferences are stored on your device. They are not uploaded to a KoBu server. Opponent names and scores are fictional offline game content, not profiles of other players.

Second chances and refreshes

The current rewarded-ad screens are local simulations. They do not contact an advertising network or show real advertisements. If a future version introduces advertising or data collection, this policy and the Google Play disclosures will be updated before that version is released.

Vibration and external links

The app uses device vibration for optional game feedback. You can turn it off in the game settings. Opening this policy or an email link launches an external browser or email app; those services operate under their own privacy policies.

Retention and deletion

Local game information remains on your device until you clear the app's storage or uninstall it. Clearing storage removes your local progress and preferences. There is no KoBu online account to delete. Your device or operating system may provide its own backup services under its own settings and terms.

Support messages

If you choose to email support, KoBu receives the email address and information you include. This information is used only to respond to your request and resolve the issue. Support correspondence is retained only as long as needed for that purpose and any applicable obligations. You may contact the same address to request deletion of support correspondence.

This website

This support page does not use advertising, analytics scripts, cookies, sign-in or contact forms. The hosting provider necessarily processes normal web requests, which may include IP address, browser information and request logs, to deliver and protect the website. These website requests are separate from the app's local game data.

Audience

The app is intended for players aged 13 and over. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent personal information to support, contact KoBu so it can be removed.

Changes and contact

Updates will be published on this page with a revised effective date. For privacy questions or requests, email koraycakmakli@hotmail.com.
